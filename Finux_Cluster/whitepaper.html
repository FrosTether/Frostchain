02. PROOF OF THERMAL WORK (PoTW)

Standard PoW encourages wasteful over-clocking. PoTW introduces a "Thermal Ceiling" consensus. Miners must validate blocks using a specific CPU percentage (60% Cap). Efficiency, not raw heat, determines the block winner. This protocol prioritizes hardware longevity and sustainable decentralization.

03. THE HALVING & BLOCK 0

Block 0 contains the Mining Reserve of 86.6M FTC. To ensure long-term scarcity, the reward of 13.37 FTC halves every 5 years and 8 months. This cycle mimics the natural growth of the REZ-HD DAG, ensuring that as more nodes join, the individual token value scales accordingly.
